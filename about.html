The Problem

Help doesn't arrive fast enough.

Across South Africa, slow or uncertain emergency response to crime, medical incidents, fires and road accidents costs lives. Many people don't know who to call, call centres are understaffed, and there's no easy way to alert family, neighbours or responders in real time.

Who do you call?

People don't know who to contact first in a crisis — family, police or neighbours — losing critical seconds to confusion.

Slow response times

Emergency response times are slow and unpredictable, leaving victims vulnerable while help is still en route.

No simultaneous alerts

There's no easy way to notify family, neighbours and emergency responders all at once with a single action.

Evidence is lost

Critical audio, video and location data disappears in the chaos, making post-incident documentation nearly impossible.
