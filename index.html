User Journey

From panic to help, in six steps

01

Trigger

Tap the SOS button — or press the power button five times if you can't unlock or speak.

02

Locate

Your live GPS location is shared instantly — no need to describe an address or landmark.

03

Connect

A response-centre operator calls back immediately, so you don't have to explain verbally.

04

Notify

Trusted family and neighbours are alerted in real time with your exact location.

05

Record

Audio and video begin recording automatically, capturing evidence from the first second.

06

Dispatch

The nearest responder is dispatched and tracked all the way to arrival.
